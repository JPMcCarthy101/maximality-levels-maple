# Source history and current limitations

The primary original computational material was prepared by the author, with assistance from Claude on some earlier files. More recent work on the last five dimensions at moment six and the consolidation of the Maple code involved ChatGPT. The historical exploratory worksheets are preserved privately by the author; they are not included in this repository because the final computations are self-contained.

**This repository is a prepared working package, not yet a public release.** Maple was not available in the assembly environment. The author reports successful execution of both new worksheets in a local Maple installation on 29 October 2026; the saved outputs and software version are now recorded in `results/`. The saved files do not record the wall-clock duration. The repository itself must accurately distinguish checks performed during assembly from the author's local Maple run.

The two current source files were constructed from the research computations, with a reconstructed moment-five driver and additions to check the exact displayed moment-six determinant, reflection, and final algebraic reductions.

Historical research notebooks are not required to verify the final results. The two distributed worksheets are standalone one-dimensional Maple code, intended for straightforward review and execution; equivalent plain-text `.mpl` sources are provided.

This package does not on its own imply a permanent DOI, or establish any software license. The source files have been checked structurally and the author has reported successful local Maple runs, but the remaining tasks are the author's mathematical/source correspondence review, a software licensing decision and explicit approval before publication.
